Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StroopBrain ("the App") handles your data.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. StroopBrain is a standalone game designed to run locally on your Apple Watch. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we track your identity, location, or personal details.

2. High Scores and Game Data

All game data, including your highest scores and round progress, are saved locally directly on your Apple Watch device. We do not transmit, store, or process this data on any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking services, or advertising networks.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Because we do not collect personal data, changes to this policy will not affect your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email provided in the Support section above.
